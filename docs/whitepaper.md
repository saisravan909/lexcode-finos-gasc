# Whitepaper: LexCode-to-CALM Architecture & Governance-as-Code

## Executive Summary
Financial institutions operate under strict, evolving regulatory mandates. Translating statutory language into operational infrastructure guardrails is historically manual, slow, and error-prone. **LexCode-to-CALM** automates this lifecycle by compiling legal domain-specific languages (DSLs) directly into **FINOS CALM (Common Architecture Language Model)** controls.

## Strategic Pillars
1. **Machine-Readable Regulation:** Legal DSLs provide version-controlled, executable obligations.
2. **Deterministic Control Generation:** Legal constraints compile directly into CALM compliance rules.
3. **Continuous Enforcement:** Fluxnova policy engines evaluate cloud topology manifests at build time.
4. **Verifiable Provenance:** Passed checks generate cryptographic audit attestations mapping directly to **FINOS Common Cloud Controls (CCC)**.
